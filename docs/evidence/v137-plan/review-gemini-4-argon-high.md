**Verdict:** **Do not approve [`docs/PLAN-V1.37.md`](../../../docs/PLAN-V1.37.md) as written.** Its floor arithmetic is right (¥10 scenery netting ¥8.42 needs 7.5–7.6% of all ~78 monthly installs, which cosmetic scenery hidden behind 25 km cannot clear) and its operational sequence (owner purchase walk → Offer v2 → Phase 2) is right for instrument calibration and customer safety. Everything built on top of those two facts is flawed: **(1)** keeping Window 1 to `N = 200` while building Phase 1 on a branch is cheap on the calendar, *except* the plan misses that `N = 122` today (`3/122 = 2.46%`) already falsifies ¥10 and ¥30 once walked, and treats a missed walk (`A′`) as an edge case after 48 days of owner non-walks; **(2)** under its own silence defaults (price stays ¥10, Phase 2 defaults to `E1` a second road inside the same SKU), the plan spends ~20+ agent-days polishing a cosmetic good it just proved cannot make ¥50/month, while burying the only viable model (a single one-time learning-depth unlock with grandfathering, `E3`, plus Japanese UI `E5` for the 53% JP install share); **(3)** Window 2's revenue instrument (`sales_report.py` `proceeds`) sums JPY, CNY, and USD without currency conversion, and its `N₂ = ⌈3/c*⌉` rule fires before its own interim checkpoint while pooling legacy-install updates into cohort conversion; and **(4)** the plan includes a crashing CLI invocation, stale gate floors, a superseded day-90 rule, unverified StoreKit/AMFI/App Review assumptions, and three breaches of the repo's own owner-only red lines.
