JLPT N5–N1 vocabulary with English & Chinese meanings, example sentences, and toggleable hints.

Smart review

Words you miss flow into a spaced-repetition queue (SM-2), so you practice exactly what you need.

📝

Practice articles

183 calm passages — from greetings to literary paragraphs — for distraction-free long-form typing.

Works offline

No account with us, no tracking, no analytics. Everything you practise works with no connection; iCloud sync is on by default, can be switched off, and uses your own iCloud.
