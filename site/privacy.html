Nihongo Ride — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

The short version

Nihongo Ride collects nothing. There is no account with us, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no server of ours anywhere. The app talks to Apple's own services on your behalf — iCloud, Game Center and the App Store — and to nothing else. We never receive your data from any of them.

Data collection

Nihongo Ride does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on any server of ours. There is no developer backend to send anything to. We — the developer — never receive any information about you or how you use the app, and we have no way to.

Every feature works with no connection at all. Three optional things use the network, and all three are Apple's, running under your own Apple Account rather than an account with us:

  • iCloud sync (on by default, switchable off). Your review schedule, ride log, odometer, saved words and word lists are copied into your own private iCloud database so your devices agree. It is your iCloud, governed by Apple's privacy policy; the developer cannot read it. Turn it off under Settings → iCloud sync and the app stops using the network for this entirely.
  • Game Center. If you are signed in to Game Center, Time Attack scores are submitted to its leaderboard. Sign out of Game Center on your device and nothing is submitted.
  • The App Store. The one optional in-app purchase is handled by the App Store through your Apple Account. We never see your payment details.

What is stored on your device

Your learning progress — which words you've practiced, your spaced-repetition review queue, your ride log, and your settings — is stored locally on your device (in the app's sandbox container). Since version 1.32 that also includes a small local tally of which kana you are most often refused on, kept so the app can show you what you keep missing; it is counts and kana, never a record of your keystrokes, and it is never synced or sent anywhere. Deleting the app removes all of it.

With iCloud sync on, the items listed above under "Data collection" are also in your own private iCloud. Nothing else leaves the device.

No third parties

  • Analytics & advertising: None. No third-party analytics or ad SDKs are included.
  • Our servers: None. There is no Nihongo Ride backend. The only network destinations are Apple's: iCloud, Game Center and the App Store.
  • AI services: None at runtime. (Learning content was prepared during development; the app itself contacts no AI service.)
  • Accounts & sign-in: No account with us — no login, no email, no identifiers of ours. iCloud sync and Game Center use the Apple Account you are already signed in to on the device.

Children's privacy

The developer collects no data at all, so there is nothing about a child for us to hold. It contains no objectionable content. Game Center leaderboards are the one place a name can be visible to other players; signing out of Game Center on the device switches that off.

Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled, this page is updated. This page was rewritten in September 2026: the earlier wording said the app made no network connections at all, which stopped being accurate when iCloud sync shipped and stayed on this page longer than it should have. Nothing about what the developer receives has ever changed — that is still nothing — but a privacy policy has to describe the app that exists.

Nihongo Ride — 隐私政策

最后更新:2026 年 9 月

一句话版本

Nihongo Ride 不收集任何数据。没有开发者账号体系,没有分析、追踪或广告,开发者也没有任何服务器。应用只会代你连接 Apple 自己的服务 —— iCloud、Game Center 和 App Store —— 除此以外不连接任何地方。我们从这些服务里也收不到你的任何数据。

数据收集

本应用不会收集、传输或在开发者的任何服务器上存储个人数据 —— 因为根本不存在这样的服务器。开发者永远不会、也无从收到关于你或你如何使用应用的任何信息。

所有功能在完全离线的情况下都能用。有三项可选功能会用到网络,而且都是 Apple 的服务,走的是你自己的 Apple 账户,而不是我们的账号:

  • iCloud 同步(默认开启,可关闭)。复习计划、骑行记录、里程、收藏词和词单会同步到你自己的私有 iCloud 数据库,让你的多台设备保持一致。那是你的 iCloud,适用 Apple 的隐私政策,开发者无法读取。在「设置 → iCloud 同步」关掉后,应用就不再为此使用网络。
  • Game Center。如果你已登录 Game Center,限时模式的成绩会提交到排行榜。在设备上退出 Game Center 即不再提交。
  • App Store。唯一那项可选内购由 App Store 通过你自己的 Apple 账户完成,我们看不到你的支付信息。

设备上保存的内容

你的学习进度(练过哪些词、间隔复习队列、骑行记录、设置项)保存在你设备本地(应用沙盒容器内)。从 1.32 版起还包括一份本地统计:你最常被拒的假名有哪些,用来告诉你"你反复错在哪"。它只有假名和次数,不是击键记录,既不同步也不外发。删除应用即清除全部这些数据。

开启 iCloud 同步后,上面「数据收集」一节列出的那几项也会存在你自己的私有 iCloud 里。除此之外没有任何东西离开设备。

无第三方

  • 分析与广告:无。不含任何第三方分析或广告 SDK。
  • 我们的服务器:无。Nihongo Ride 没有后端。应用唯一会连接的目的地是 Apple 的服务:iCloud、Game Center、App Store。
  • AI 服务:运行时无。(学习内容在开发阶段准备完成;应用本身不连接任何 AI 服务。)
  • 账号与登录:没有我们的账号 —— 无登录、无邮箱、无我们的任何标识符。iCloud 同步与 Game Center 使用你设备上已登录的 Apple 账户。
