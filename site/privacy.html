Nihongo Ride — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

The short version

Nihongo Ride collects nothing. It has no account system, makes no network connections, and contains no analytics, tracking, or advertising. Everything you do stays on your Mac.

Data collection

Nihongo Ride does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on any server. The app is fully offline. We — the developer — never receive any information about you or how you use the app.

What is stored on your device

Your learning progress — which words you've practiced, your spaced-repetition review queue, and your settings — is stored locally on your Mac (in the app's sandbox container). This data never leaves your device. Deleting the app removes it.

No third parties

  • Analytics & advertising: None. No third-party analytics or ad SDKs are included.
  • External servers: None. The app does not connect to the internet.
  • AI services: None at runtime. (Learning content was prepared during development; the app itself contacts no AI service.)
  • Accounts & sign-in: None. No login, no email, no identifiers.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data at all, it is safe for users of any age. It contains no objectionable content.

Changes

If a future version ever adds a feature that changes how data is handled (for example, optional iCloud sync handled entirely by Apple), this page will be updated before that version ships.

Nihongo Ride — 隐私政策

最后更新:2026 年 6 月

一句话版本

Nihongo Ride 不收集任何数据。没有账号系统,不联网,不含任何分析、追踪或广告。你的一切操作都只留在你的 Mac 上。

数据收集

本应用不会收集、传输或在任何服务器上存储任何个人数据。应用完全离线运行,开发者也永远不会收到关于你或你如何使用应用的任何信息。

设备上保存的内容

你的学习进度(练过哪些词、间隔复习队列、设置项)仅保存在你 Mac 本地(应用沙盒容器内),永不离开设备。删除应用即清除这些数据。

无第三方

  • 分析与广告:无。不含任何第三方分析或广告 SDK。
  • 外部服务器:无。应用不联网。
  • AI 服务:运行时无。(学习内容在开发阶段准备完成;应用本身不连接任何 AI 服务。)
  • 账号与登录:无。无登录、无邮箱、无任何标识符。
